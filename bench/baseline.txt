lightbox timing baseline

Taken before Phase 1 of ROADMAP.md changed how a container starts.

  tree       commit 2320a8d, lightc v0.1.1
  measured   2026-11-14, GitHub Actions run 43645667727, `sudo make bench`
  machines   GitHub-hosted runners ubuntu-24.04 and ubuntu-24.04-arm,
             4 processors each, Linux 7.0.0-1017-azure
  image      the lifecycle suite's root filesystem: one static busybox

Wall time in milliseconds, as median (lowest to highest) of 10 runs.

  command            x86_64                    aarch64
  create              16.6 (12.9 to 166.7)      10.7 (10.5 to 11.3)
  start              534.8 (527.7 to 658.3)    541.6 (535.5 to 553.2)
  exec /bin/true       7.2 (3.4 to 10.4)        10.7 (6.7 to 16.6)
  stop               206.7 (206.5 to 230.1)    207.9 (207.3 to 208.3)
  rm                   5.2 (5.1 to 5.4)          4.2 (4.0 to 4.5)

  processes made by one start: 45 on both

Reading the numbers

  start   500 ms of it are a fixed wait before lightbox looks whether init is
          alive (RT-ready-sleep). The work takes the other 35 to 40 ms.
  stop    lightbox signals init and looks again after 200 ms, in steps of
          200 ms (PERF-stop-poll). An idle container is gone at the first look.
  45      the lightbox command, the container's init, and 43 tools they run
          (PERF-start-spawns): 9 times ip or nsenter on the host's side, 15
          times the host's mount before the pivot, and 19 times a tool of the
          image after it. The lifecycle suite counted those 19 in the same
          run: mount 18 times, ip once.

These are shared machines, so the spread is theirs as much as lightbox's: a
later number is worth comparing only when it comes from the same kind of
runner, and only when the difference is larger than the spread above. The
process count does not vary.
